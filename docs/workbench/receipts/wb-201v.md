- The seam. `dispatchFunctionWithAuthority` decides when a function request enters. It awaits the admission wait, then decides again from fresh host reads, and dispatches at most once on that final decision. Nothing is awaited between the final decision and the dispatch. A request refused at entry never waits. `decideFunctionAuthority` is the pure decision, and its rules apply in this order: a function definition; a known effective principal and session, unchanged since entry; known permission data; `workbench:execute`; an adopted action for the definition; that exact action grant, matched by string equality. Grants and identity come from the host's two reads only. The request, its input, its JSON policy and the adopted binding of the reviewed definition confer nothing.
- Explicitly partial. No action spelling is adopted, so `FUNCTION_ACTION_CONTRACT` is `{ state: "unadopted", blockedOn: "MW-CONTRACT" }` and every function dispatch refuses as `function-action-unadopted`. The adopted contracts in the suite are test fixtures (`fixture-fn:*`), not a proposed spelling. `adoptFunctionActions` throws on any spelling that `workbench.capabilityGrants` (namespace:permission, no wildcard) or the detached launch request (at most 256 characters per grant) cannot carry. It also throws on a `workbench:` grant and on a definition or grant bound twice. Only a contract it returned is honored. The program's example spelling, `mechanical:develop:write` (`02-host-architecture-and-protocol.md` line 52 at `0b25e14e`), has two colons: `loadSettings` refuses it, and so does adoption. The external remainder is parked on this bead for MW-CONTRACT.
