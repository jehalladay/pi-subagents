- The seam. `dispatchFunctionWithAuthority` decides when a function request enters. It awaits the admission wait, then decides again from fresh host reads, and dispatches at most once on that final decision. Nothing is awaited between the final decision and the dispatch. A request refused at entry never waits. `decideFunctionAuthority` is the pure decision, and its rules apply in this order: a function definition; a known effective principal and session, unchanged since entry; known permission data; `workbench:execute`; an adopted action for the definition; that exact action grant, matched by string equality. Grants and identity come from the host's two reads only. The request, its input, its JSON policy and the adopted binding of the reviewed definition confer nothing.
