## Authority and boundary
MechanicalWorkflows owns pure coordination composition, typed Intents and deterministic reconcile/admission; native hosts own effects. GraphPolicyCLI is an external optional pure adapter for scoring observed agent/task/resource/artifact/capability graphs. Here *pure* means: a function of (GraphDataIR, program/checkpoint hash, budget) only, reading or writing no source host, invoked by the host adapter outside `Policy.propose` (which may spawn no process). When GP is absent, unavailable, over budget or abstains, the host uses the existing named fallback policy and records the fallback reason; coordination never depends on GP. It neither schedules work nor claims tool effects, and does not reinterpret mw.training-export.v1 post-proposal admission as the policy's pre-sampling eligible support.

## Proposal pipeline
1. Host pins source-owned graph state and observation completeness/provenance (TARGET: at HEAD 19ca8d4 provenance is derived from proposals, not from the observation, and completeness can only be recorded as the literal "unknown").
2. A trusted adapter (TARGET; no such adapter exists at HEAD 19ca8d4), given capacity/permission inputs the host supplies (today's `PolicyContext` carries episodeId, actorId, observationDigest, budget, authority, policyRevision and seed — no capacity or permission inputs beyond budget and authority) and a GraphDataIR defined by GraphPolicyCLI (external, unpinned), enumerates a finite, typed, legal **pre-sampling** assignment support under the capacity/permission constraints the adapter is handed (tiny synthetic graphs first); constraints not known at proposal time remain `reconcile`'s to enforce. It exports a bounded role-aware GraphDataIR.
3. GP is specified (TARGET; no implementation exists) to return keyed agent/task pair logits or a graph value with program/checkpoint/snapshot hash, finite resource budget and explicit abstain status (this program's requirement on GP; GP's core program as read 2026-10-18 does not specify an abstain status).
4. A recorded distribution samples only from actual eligible support (TARGET: at HEAD 19ca8d4 the only `Selector<O>` chooses "left"|"right" and every exported decision carries `logProb: "unknown"`; no channel records a behavior log probability), or a deterministic optimizer converts scores into a globally valid set of assignments; independent per-pair argmax is not sufficient for capacity constraints.
5. Pure mapping creates candidate Intent using current expected basis (CURRENT: `graphPolicyAdapter` pins `expectedRevision` to the batch `Basis.revision`). MechanicalWorkflows reconcile independently accepts/rejects (CURRENT). Existing host executes admitted actions (CURRENT: the single live `gate.run` flow).
6. A sidecar — one named, versioned schema (`mw.decision-receipt.v1`, CURRENT: packages/coordination/schema/decision-receipt.v1.schema.json is the single home of the field list; neither this overview nor the README enumerates fields) — correlates decision, admission and outcome records (today: seeded gate draw / tape digest in fixture and replay episodes and the single live `gate.run` flow; a general live verifier channel and an episode schema slot are TARGET). Feedback corrections and promotion remain outside the policy's evaluation.

## Experiments and falsifiers
First run synthetic landing queue and heterogeneous DAG assignment, including stale head, worker failure, scarce resources, task conflict and temporal shifts. Compare the canonical arm list: FIFO/restack and greedy/static-role heuristics ('greedy' exists only as an inline test policy; 'static role' does not exist), an exact small-instance oracle (hypothesis: brute-force exact matching is tractable at the chosen n <= N; N and the regret definition are fixed before the run — neither exists today), a graph-feature MLP (unbuilt), a MicroPolicy feature-only scorer (unbuilt), a GP relational scorer (unbuilt), and optional GP→MP composition (unbuilt). Split by graph-family/episode, record p95 total cost, verified throughput, regret and invalid proposals (TARGET: `mw.evaluation.v1` at HEAD 19ca8d4 records p95Steps and mean compute with a seed-based holdout and refuses mixed families with EVALUATION_BATCH_UNMATCHED; regret, constraint violations, wall-clock tail cost, verified throughput and a per-family split are new). For this program, 'classical matching' means the queue-heuristic arm (fifo/restack/greedy), not the exact oracle; promotion requires strictly better quality on a named metric at a stated threshold with no worse overhead; parity does not promote.

## Deliverables
Versioned projection/decision receipt sidecar, fixture tests, shadow-mode adapter, conformance and authority tests, then opt-in canary with rollback. No existing behavioral guarantees or training-export semantics are changed by this document.

CURRENT (2026-10-21): the development rig ships a bounded shadow/canary bead-selection host (`--select-bead`, [docs/16](../16-graph-policy-bead-selection.md)) that invokes the real GraphPolicyCLI binary over a host-enumerated pre-sampling support and records the `mw.decision-receipt.v1` sidecar; one canary decision with a native Beads claim was witnessed. The design statements above are unchanged by it: no training, promotion or learned improvement exists.
